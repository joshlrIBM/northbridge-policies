# Northbridge Bank Wire Transfer Review Standard

*Fictional document created for an InstructLab demo. Northbridge Bank is not a real bank.*

Policy ID: WRS-4
Owner: Compliance Operations
Effective: October 1, 2026

## 1. Purpose

The Wire Transfer Review Standard (WRS-4) sets the review steps Northbridge Bank analysts must complete before releasing outgoing client wire transfers. It applies to all relationship managers and Compliance Operations analysts.

## 2. Definitions

A first-time beneficiary is any beneficiary the client has not successfully wired funds to in the previous 12 months.

An international wire is any wire where the beneficiary's bank is located outside the United States.

## 3. International wires to first-time beneficiaries

Any international wire of $10,000 or more to a first-time beneficiary requires enhanced review. Enhanced review has three requirements:

1. A second-analyst sign-off from a Compliance Operations analyst who did not prepare the wire.
2. A 24-hour hold, starting from the time the wire request is submitted.
3. A completed Form NB-207, the Beneficiary Verification Form.

International wires under $10,000 to a first-time beneficiary need a completed Form NB-207, but no second-analyst sign-off and no hold.

## 4. Domestic wires

Domestic wires of $50,000 or more to a first-time beneficiary require a callback verification. No hold is required for domestic wires.

## 5. Callback verification

Every enhanced review and every domestic callback must be verified by calling the client at the phone number on file in the client's Northbridge profile. Analysts must never use a phone number provided in the wire request itself.

## 6. Restricted countries

If the beneficiary's bank is in a country on the Northbridge Restricted Country List, the wire must be escalated to the BSA Officer, and the hold extends from 24 hours to 72 hours.

## 7. Early release

A wire on hold can be released early only with approval from the VP of Compliance Operations. The approval must be recorded in Section 4 of Form NB-207.

## 8. Recordkeeping

Completed Form NB-207 records and second-analyst sign-offs must be kept for five years.
